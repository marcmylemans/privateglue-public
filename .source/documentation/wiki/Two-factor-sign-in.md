# Two-factor sign-in

With two-factor sign-in on, your password alone is not enough. After the password you type a six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. Someone who learns your password still cannot get in.

Any authenticator app works, including the one built into a password manager. No phone number and no outside service are involved.

## Turn it on

1. Open the menu with your name at the top right and choose **Profile & password**.
2. Under **Two-factor sign-in**, click **Set up**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or type the key shown next to it.
4. Type the code the app shows and click **Turn on two-factor sign-in**.
5. **Save the ten recovery codes** that appear. They are shown once.

Turning it on signs out your other devices.

## Recovery codes

A recovery code signs you in when your phone is not at hand. On the code page, choose **Use a recovery code**.

- Each code works once.
- Your profile shows how many are left. **New recovery codes** makes ten new ones and cancels the old ones.
- Keep them somewhere other than your phone, for example with your backups.

## Signing in

After your password you get the **Enter your code** page.

- A code is valid for about 30 seconds and works once. The clock of your phone has to be roughly right.
- Five wrong codes, or five minutes of waiting, send you back to the password.

## Turn it off

In your profile, **Turn off** asks for your password and a code. A recovery code works too.

## For administrators

### Require it

Under **Users**, the **Two-factor sign-in** panel sets who must use it: nobody, administrators or everyone. The table shows who has it on.

Requiring it never locks anyone out. People it applies to can still sign in with their password, and are sent to the set-up page until they have finished it.

### Someone lost their phone

Open the person under **Users** and click **Reset two-factor sign-in**. They then sign in with their password alone and can set it up again. You cannot reset your own from there: use **Turn off** in your profile.

### Do not lock yourself out

If the only administrator loses both the phone and the recovery codes, nobody in the app can reset them. Keep your recovery codes safe, and have a second administrator account.

If it happens anyway: restore your latest backup into a fresh install. Two-factor sign-in is part of the backup, so you still need a recovery code or the authenticator from that time.
